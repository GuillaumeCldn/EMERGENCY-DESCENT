\chapter{Executive summary}
% ===============================================================

This document is the functional specification of the EMERGENCY DESCENT function. It provides “aircraft-level” requirements that will be the main input for systems specifications.

The purpose of the EMERGENCY DESCENT function is to have the aircraft descend to a safe altitude in the case of loss of cabin pressurisation.

\chapter{Function Description}
\label{sec:FD}
% ===============================================================

The purpose of the EMERGENCY DESCENT function is to navigate the aircraft from its current altitude to \SI{10000}{ft} or the MSA, following the fastest safe descent profile.

The EMERGENCY DESCENT function provides the following features:

\begin{itemize}
	\item It allows the flight crew to activate the EMERGENCY DESCENT. 
	\item Upon activation of the EMERGENCY DESCENT:
		\subitem The aircraft begins descending to \SI{10000}{ft}, or the published MSA (whichever is greater) at \SI{250}{kts} IAS.
		\subitem It allows the flight crew to select a desired altitude if different from \SI{10000}{ft}. 
		\subitem It allows the flight crew to set a desired IAS for the descent. 
	\item Upon arriving to the selected altitude, the aircraft levels off in ALT mode: the EMERGENCY DESCENT function is finished. 
	\item It allows the flight crew to deactivate the EMERGENCY DESCENT, in which case the aircraft levels off at its current altitude.
\end{itemize}

\chapter{Operational Scenarios}
\label{sec:OS}
% ===============================================================

In nominal scenario 1, it is an emergency descent with structural damage.

In nominal scenario 2, it is an emergency descent without structural damage.

\section{Nominal scenario 1}

\textbf{Initial conditions:} The aircraft is flying at a typical A320 cruise altitude, both pilots are in the
cockpit, the autopilot is engaged. An explosive depressurization occurs resulting in
structural damage to the aircraft.

\textbf{STEP 1 :} A visual and an audio alert inform the pilots of the situation. The pilots put
on their oxygen mask.

\textbf{STEP 2 :} The system sets the altitude to \SI{10000}{ft} and the speed to \SI{250}{kts} ; and sets
the heading to Selected Mode. There is an audio announcement of these actions,
and the information is displayed on the FCU. The SPD/MACH knob is set to SPD.
Terrain mode is displayed on the ND. The actions will be active (Managed Mode) after a
delay (few seconds). Meanwhile,  pilots can still modify the instructions before they become
active.

\textbf{STEP 3 :} the system alerts the air traffic controller of the situation (Emergency depressurization and Emergency descent) + target altitude and speed (+ vertical speed ?).

\textbf{STEP 4 :} pilots manage the systems and communications with ATC. They deploy
the speedbrakes as needed.


\section{Nominal scenario 2}

\textbf{Initial conditions:} The aircraft is flying at a typical A320 cruise altitude, both pilots are in the
cockpit, the autopilot is engaged. An explosive depressurization occurs without
structural damage to the aircraft.

\textbf{STEP 1 :} A visual and audio alert informs the pilots of the situation. The pilots put on
their oxygen mask.

\textbf{STEP 2 :} The system sets the altitude to \SI{10000}{ft} and the speed to \SI{250}{kts} ; and sets
the heading to Selected Mode. There is an audio announcement of these actions,
and the information is displayed on the FCU. The SPD/MACH knob is set to SPD.
Terrain mode is displayed on the ND. The actions will be active (Managed Mode) after a
delay. Meanwhile, pilots can still modify the instructions before they become 
active.

\textbf{STEP 3 :} pilots set speed to selected mode and increase it to VMO.

\textbf{STEP 4 :} the system alerts the air traffic controller of the situation (Emergency
 depressurization and Emergency descent) + target altitude and speed (+ vertical speed ?).

\textbf{STEP 5 :} pilots manage the systems and communications with ATC. They deploy
the speed brakes as needed.

\chapter{Requirements}
\label{sec:reqs}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{REQ-\arabic* :}, leftmargin=2cm]
    \item The system shall enable the aircraft to reach an altitude of 10000~ft without any intervention of the pilot in less than \textit{TBD} minutes.
    \item The system shall detect the presence of structural damage and adapt the managed speed accordingly.
    \item In case of structural damage, the system shall maintain the managed speed of the aircraft at 250~kts.
    \item In case of the absence of any structural damage, the system shall maintain the managed speed of the aircraft at V\textsubscript{MO}.
    \item The system shall set the HDG to Selected Mode, the SPD/MACH to SPD and display TERR on the ND.
    \item Pilots shall be visually and audibly informed of each action the system is performing.
    \item Pilots shall be able to cancel any spurious activation of the system in less than \textit{TBD} seconds and with \textit{TBD} buttons only (i.e., must be easy to control).
    \item Pilots shall be able to modify any parameter set by the system in less than \textit{TBD} seconds and with \textit{TBD} buttons only (i.e., must be easy to control).
    \item The system shall inform the ATCO of the EMER DES state of the aircraft, and the target parameters.
\end{enumerate}
